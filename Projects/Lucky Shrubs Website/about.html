About Us

We are a passionate team of gardeners and landscapers dedicated to transforming outdoor spaces into beautiful, thriving environments.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Lucky Shrub started as a small family business with a love for plants. Over the years we have grown into a trusted local landscaping company serving hundreds of happy customers across the region.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create beautiful, sustainable outdoor spaces that bring joy to our clients. We combine creativity with expertise to deliver results that exceed expectations on every single project.

Our Team

Our team of certified landscapers and horticulturalists bring decades of combined experience to every project. We are passionate about what we do and it shows in every garden we create.
